# itch.io game page content

## Short description / tagline

Play the monster. Torch the hamlet, rob it blind, and scurry home before His Eternal Darkness
judges the raid.

## Description

**You're not the hero. You're the raid.**

Your Overlord — an unseen voice of layered whispers and subtitles — sends you through a runic
portal into a dusk-lit forest outside a small, unwalled human hamlet. You're a goblin. Burn the
place, rob it blind, and get your clan home before the county gets organized. Played straight,
it's a raid game. Played honestly, it's a satire — the humans here are pompous, greedy, and a
little bit stupid, and they've built exactly the kind of undefended little kingdom that deserves
a goblin problem.

**How a raid goes**

You arrive with three targets named on the spot: burn the market, bring down the statue, and take
the windmill apart. Case the hamlet, work the crowd, and when you're ready — go loud. Sound the
horn and your horde pours out of the Warren behind you: up to ten goblins at your back, following
orders, throwing themselves at whatever you point them at. Fire spreads on its own once it starts
— across fields, into buildings, through anything that'll take it — so the shape of your raid
becomes visible in the land itself, not just a checklist. Finish the three, and a portal opens.
Get out, toss what you've got into it, and hear the Overlord's verdict. You've got thirty minutes.
The portal doesn't wait.

**What actually kills you if you're careless:** five lives, a garrison that can reinforce itself
exactly once at real cost if the bell ever rings, and a clan of goblins who are agile, fast, and
still no match for a real fight if you throw them at one carelessly.

**The moment-to-moment feel:** sword-and-bow live-swap combat, a grapple hook that's a real combat
verb (not a traversal gimmick — it's how you bring the statue down), climbing and vaulting to get
somewhere the humans can't easily follow, and a horde that goes where you point it and burns what
you tell it to.

**This build.** Goblin Siege is in active development — this is a work-in-progress raid, not a
finished release. The core loop (raid, burn, extract, get scored) is playable start to finish
right now; the team is in an optimization pass, not a bug-fixing one. Expect rough edges. Feedback
is very welcome.
