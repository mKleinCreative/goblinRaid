You only see what's in line of sight — the map is dark until you explore it. Guards can't see through walls: move quietly and they stay unaware, but attacking, casting, or exploding barrels makes noise that draws them, and being spotted rings the alarm for the whole district. Enemies telegraph their strikes — dodge through melee, step out of archer aim-lines. Torches stick where they land and fire spreads; friendly fire is on (reduced). Once razed, the district's barracks fall silent but outside patrols march in and escalate. 5 lives each.
